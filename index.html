fomo is the social trading app built for the next generation — real-time feeds, leaderboards, and a community that makes trading feel like something you do together, not alone. The Campus Team is how that community gets built at your school: a small group of students who run fomo's presence on campus, from Greek Wars to everyday growth.

This isn't a one-off gig. Campus Team members get direct access to the fomo team, real responsibility, and a role that means something on a résumé — because you're not just repping an app, you're helping build a company.
