Who We Want

You don't need followers. You need energy.

We're not looking for influencers — we're looking for connectors. The person whose group chat always knows the plan first.

  • Currently enrolled full-time at a U.S. college or university
  • Active and plugged into campus social life (Greek life, clubs, sports, RA — all great)
  • Comfortable posting on your own social channels
  • Reliable, organized, and excited to plan a few events per semester
  • Bonus: experience with content creation, event planning, or marketing

Time Commitment

~4–6 hours per week

Term Length

One semester, renewable

Compensation

Stipend + bonuses + perks
